# ⚔️ Eternity Pixel Dungeon — Definitive Feature Guide

**Eternity Pixel Dungeon** is a profound and ambitious evolution of the classic turn-based tactical roguelike. Building upon the genre's traditional mechanical depth, it expands the experience into its own universe of content: new hero classes, loyal tactical companions, infinite 64-bit numerical scaling, divine magic and stealth mechanics, global cloud leaderboards, and an original audiovisual layer.

---

## 🌟 1. What Makes Eternity Unique (Core Differentiators)

Unlike *Vanilla Pixel Dungeon*, *Shattered Pixel Dungeon*, and other community forks, **Eternity Pixel Dungeon** stands apart through the following fundamental design pillars:

| Feature | Traditional Pixel Dungeon | Eternity Pixel Dungeon |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Classes** | 4 – 5 Classes | **8 Full Classes** (+ Cleric, Barbarian & playable Rat King) |
| **Companions / Pets** | Temporary / limited summons | **Persistent Pet System** with level progression, stats & tactical panel |
| **Scaling Architecture** | Standard 32-bit integer limit (`int`) | **Infinite 64-bit scaling (`long`)** with zero overflow risk |
| **Competition** | Local device records only | **Global Hall of Fame & Leaderboards** synced to the cloud |
| **Cross-Platform & Licensing** | Store-isolated purchases | **Universal Cross-License (`EPD-GOLD-...`)** across PC, Steam & Mobile |
| **Soundtrack & Visuals** | Standard MIDI / chiptune audio | **Original Soundtrack (OST)** and high-definition proprietary art |
| **Monetization** | Often contains ads or energy walls | **100% Ad-Free**, no stamina limits, zero Pay-to-Win |

## 🛡️ 2. The Expanded Hero Roster (8 Classes)

Each class features unique starting equipment, deep talent trees (Tiers 1 through 4), and two specialized subclasses offering contrasting playstyles:

1. **Warrior**
   - *Subclasses:* **Berserker** (massive damage scaling when enduring punishment) and **Gladiator** (devastating martial combo chains).
   - *Role:* Physical endurance, frontline melee mastery, and transferable armor seals.
2. **Mage**
   - *Subclasses:* **Battlemage** (infuses melee strikes with staff powers) and **Warlock** (marks enemy souls to siphon health and satisfy hunger).
   - *Role:* Arcane supremacy, fast wand recharging, and ranged elemental artillery.
3. **Rogue**
   - *Subclasses:* **Assassin** (lethal, armor-piercing strikes from the shadows) and **Freerunner** (superhuman evasion fueled by high momentum).
   - *Role:* Cloak of shadows, trap evasion, stealth ambushes, and unmatched mobility.
4. **Huntress**
   - *Subclasses:* **Sniper** (pinpoint long-distance shots ignoring armor) and **Warden** (deep synergy with plant life, dew, and protective furrow).
   - *Role:* Spirit bow with infinite magical ammo, plant vision, and ranged zone control.
5. **Duelist**
   - *Subclasses:* **Champion** (simultaneous dual-wielding mastery) and **Monk** (inner focus, unarmed combos, and meditative recovery).
   - *Role:* Specialized weapon abilities and fluid stance-swapping combat.
6. **Cleric — Exclusive to Eternity!**
   - *Subclasses:* **Paladin** (armored holy crusader bolstered by divine blessings) and **Priest** (conduit of celestial radiance, healing, and undead eradication).
   - *Unique Mechanic:* Faith and Divine Devotion through the **Holy Tome**, protective prayers, and smiting celestial judgments.
7. **Barbarian — Exclusive to Eternity!**
   - *Subclasses:* **Warmonger** (unstoppable, bloodthirsty juggernaut) and **Beastmaster** (feral resonance with supreme companion command).
   - *Unique Mechanic:* Rage management that ramps attack velocity and reduces incoming damage during berserk surges.
8. **Rat King — Secret Playable Hero!**
   - *Subclass:* **King**.
   - *Unique Mechanics:* Legacy Wrath powers, transmogrifying foes (*Ratmogrify*), and dominion over dungeon vermin.

## 🐾 3. Deep Tactical Pet & Companion System

Pets in Eternity are not mere cosmetic followers; they are true tactical allies featuring independent experience, stat progression, and active combat abilities:

- **Pet Eggs (*Pet Egg*)**: Found hidden within secret alcoves and nests throughout the dungeon, ready to be incubated and nurtured during your descent.
- **Tactical Command Panel (*PetTacticalPanel*)**: Command your pet in real time (Aggressive attack mode, Defensive guard, Tactical retreat, or Manual ability execution).
- **Pet Whistle (*PetWhistle*)**: Quick-recall tool for coordinating synchronized pincer maneuvers.
- **Companion Roster:**
  - 🐉 **Dragon**: Breathes searing flames that immolate enemy clusters with area-of-effect burn damage.
  - 🐺 **Wolf**: Lightning-quick gap closing, flanking maneuvers, and vicious critical bite strikes.
  - 🧚 **Fairy**: Continuous support providing healing blessings, magical shielding, and clairvoyant vision.
  - 🕷️ **Spider**: Shoots immobilizing webbing, applies potent neurotoxins, and locks down corridor choke points.
  - 🦁 **Manticore (Supporter / Gold Exclusive)**: Colossal winged beast wielding a deadly paralyzing stinger and armor-shredding claw swipes.

## ♾️ 4. Extended Infinite Engine (64-Bit `long` Architecture)

Designed for roguelike veterans craving endless challenge, deep loops, and uncapped progression:
- **Beyond 32-bit Limitations**: Core game calculations (damage formulas, health pools, gold economy, and experience curve) run on 64-bit `long` types.
- **Infinite Run Capability**: Chain victories together, upgrade gear to extraordinary heights (+50, +100 and beyond), and face escalating monster hordes without numerical overflow, rollbacks, or system freezes.

## 🏺 5. Exclusive Items, Advanced Alchemy & Secret Vaults

- **Proprietary Weapons & Projectiles**:
  - *Shadow Shuriken (ShurikenOfShadows)*: High-tier missile designed to maximize surprise attacks from stealth.
  - *Clayball*: Tactical throwing item that hinders enemy movement and breaks formation.
  - *Flashbang*: Crafted via alchemy to blind, stun, and disorient entire rooms of high-threat foes.
- **Expanded Curse Infusion (*CurseInfusion*)**:
  - High-risk, high-reward enchantment system granting equipment immense upgrade levels and dark perk traits if the player learns to master corrupted artifacts.
- **The Imp’s Secret Vault (*Imp Quest Vault*)**:
  - A procedurally generated trial chamber where the hero is stripped of standard gear and must conquer the labyrinth using raw strategy and an emergency warp crystal.

## 🌐 6. Cloud Ecosystem, Hall of Fame & Cross-Platform Play

- **Global Online Leaderboards (Hall of Fame)**:
  - Backed by secure cloud infrastructure to record your deepest descents, completion times, hero classes, and all-time high scores against players worldwide.
- **Universal Cross-Licensing (`EPD-GOLD-...`)**:
  - Unlock or purchase Gold Supporter status on one platform (Steam, Itch.io, or Google Play Android), and redeem your key across all your other devices without purchasing twice.
- **True Multi-Platform Support**:
  - **Windows PC**: Lightweight, ultra-fast native `.exe` with zero installation required.
  - **Universal Desktop**: Cross-platform `.jar` running flawlessly on Linux, macOS, and Steam Deck.
  - **Android**: Available on the Google Play Store and standalone APK with full touch-optimized controls.

## 🎨 7. Audiovisual Identity & Atmospheric Polish

- **Original Orchestrated Soundtrack (OST)**: Bespoke, high-fidelity music composed specifically for each of the dungeon's five biomes (Sewers, Prison, Caves, Dwarven Metropolis, and Demon Halls).
- **Proprietary Pixel Art Layer**: Handcrafted boss sprites, atmospheric spell lighting, radiant golden UI frames for supporters, and a modern, responsive interface.
- **Complete 22-Language Localization**: Full translation parity across all 22 supported languages, ensuring zero missing keys or broken formatting.

## 💡 Why Is It So Entertaining?

Eternity Pixel Dungeon blends the classic tension of **permadeath**—where every decision carries weight—with **unmatched tactical freedom**:
- **Infinite Variety**: Dungeon layouts, enemy champions, potion identities, and scroll types are dynamically shuffled in every run.
- **Class & Pet Synergy**: Pairing 8 hero classes with 5 distinct companion archetypes creates hundreds of viable builds (e.g., a Paladin Cleric drawing enemy fire while his Dragon rains fireballs from the backline, or a stealth Assassin Rogue timing backstabs with a stalking Wolf).
- **Skill-Based Mastery**: Unforgiving yet fair; victory is earned through knowledge of monster mechanics, inventory foresight, and spatial positioning.
